Core Features

The Focus Engine

Forget rigid timers. Beacon lets you define exactly what you want to achieve, pick a profile tailored for the task (like Deep Work or Casual Study), and seamlessly layer Pomodoro intervals on top. It’s an engine designed to pull you into a flow state, rather than just counting down minutes on a screen.

Smart Interventions

We believe in intent, not imprisonment. When you drift toward a distraction, Beacon pauses you with a gentle warning page—giving you a moment to breathe and reconsider. If you absolutely need a hard stop, our native MV3 integration cuts network access instantly.

Context-Aware Modes

The modern web isn't black and white. Beacon understands context. Need to watch a React tutorial on YouTube? No problem. But if you try to open YouTube Shorts or the homepage feed, Beacon steps in. Allow specific Subreddits while blocking the doom-scroll.

Your Data, Your Machine

Privacy isn't a feature; it's the foundation. There are no accounts to create, no servers syncing your habits, and absolutely no telemetry. Everything Beacon learns about your focus patterns stays encrypted locally on your browser. Export it yourself anytime.
